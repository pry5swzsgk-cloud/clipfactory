Clipfactory Entwuerfe

Clipfactory Entwuerfe is a small personal desktop tool. It sends short video clips that the account owner has edited on their own computer to their own TikTok account as drafts, using TikTok's official Content Posting API (upload to inbox). Nothing is published automatically. The account owner opens each draft in the TikTok app and decides whether to post it.

The tool is used only by its owner for their own TikTok account. It is not offered to other users.
