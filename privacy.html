Privacy Policy – Clipfactory Entwuerfe

Last updated: 25 November 2026

1. Summary

Clipfactory Entwuerfe is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner for their own TikTok account. It has no server, no user database and no analytics. All data stays on the owner's computer.

2. Data the tool handles

  • TikTok access data: access token, refresh token, the account's open ID and the granted permissions (scopes). They are received from TikTok after the owner signs in and are stored only in a local file on the owner's computer.
  • Basic profile information: the account's display name (scope user.info.basic), used only to show which account is connected.
  • Videos: video files the owner chooses to upload. They are sent directly to TikTok as drafts (scope video.upload).
  • Upload log: date, file name, TikTok publish ID and status of each upload, stored locally.

3. Purpose

The data is used only to upload the owner's videos to their TikTok account as drafts and to check the upload status.

4. Sharing

Data is sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official API. It is not sold or shared with anyone else.

5. Retention and deletion

Access data is kept until the owner signs out. Signing out revokes the access at TikTok and deletes the local file. Access can also be revoked at any time in the TikTok account settings.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
